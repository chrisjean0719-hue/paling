# Roadmap

Paling plans one picket panel over HTTP and counts its pickets and wood. Five updates remain, and about 38 hours stay after those.

## The next five updates

### Gravel board (the next update)

Add a board under the pickets. The pickets shorten by its height, and the board is one extra stick.

### Pointed tops

Add a point on each picket and count that extra wood.

### Posts

Place a post at each end, with a length set below the panel.

### Elevation

Return the panel as an SVG, with the pickets, the gaps, and the rails.

### Compare

Compare two panels by picket count and wood length.

## After the sixth update

These stay after the six updates. They are about 38 hours.

### Gate leaf (4 hours)

Plan a matching gate in one bay and count its extra stiles.

### Post concrete (3 hours)

Estimate the concrete for each post from a hole size.

### Arris rail (3 hours)

Choose a rectangular or an arris rail and report the rail section.

### Stain area (4 hours)

Turn the faces of the pickets and the rails into a stain area.

### Bay count (4 hours)

Repeat the panel across a run and count the shared posts once.

### Slope (4 hours)

Step the pickets down a slope while keeping each picket a whole length.

### Nail count (3 hours)

Count the nails where each picket crosses a rail.

### Post spacing (4 hours)

Check that the posts stay inside a chosen maximum spacing.

### Print sheet (3 hours)

Lay the counts and the elevation out for one printed page.

### Capping (3 hours)

Add a cap along the posts and report its length.

### Hardware (3 hours)

List the nails, the clips, and the post caps for the plan.

A gravel board is the next piece. The API can plan one panel, and it does not yet add a gravel board.
